    About me

    Hello! I am a second-year PhD student in Electrical Engineering in the Communications Theory and Systems track at the University of California San Diego, advised by Prof. Rahul Parhi. I received my BSc in Electrical Engineering, with a focus on communications, from the University of Tehran.

    My research interests broadly lie in Probability theory, universality, optimization, and signal processing.
